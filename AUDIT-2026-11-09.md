shutdown wiring, one functional dead-end in music retry, two lock-contention
hotspots, and CI/publish gating.

### Follow-up bugfix (2026-11-10): punctuated titles returned no releases

A show title ending in punctuation (for example "Am I the Asshole?") was sent
verbatim to tracker search forms as `q=Am I the Asshole? S02E02`. IPTorrents and
XSpeeds match literal tokens, so the `?` produced zero results for both the
pre-filled and automatic searches, while a manually typed title without it
worked. `trackerQuery` now removes only query-breaking characters (`?`, `#`) and
apostrophes before building the provider query, while the original query still
drives result matching. `normalizeSearchText` also drops apostrophes so
"Carr's" matches releases written "Carrs". Meaningful characters such as `&`
are preserved, keeping the exact-alias Anime contract. Regression tests:
`TestDirectSearchStripsTitlePunctuationForTrackerQueries` and
`TestNormalizeSearchTextMatchesPunctuatedTitles`.
